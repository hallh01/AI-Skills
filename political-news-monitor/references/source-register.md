# Political news source register
Use these as *source categories*, not as evidence of a current event.
- Official: congress.gov, federalregister.gov, whitehouse.gov, state legislatures, courts, election administrators, foreign ministries, intergovernmental bodies.
- Public research: official statistical agencies, academic institutions and journals.
- Reporting: two independent reputable outlets with named reporting and transparent corrections.
For every item record: title, organization, primary/secondary, URL, event date, publication date, retrieval date, independent corroboration, and caveats.
